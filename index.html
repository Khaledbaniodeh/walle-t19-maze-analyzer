04

Explore · Return · Race

Phase metrics appear after a log is analyzed.

Counts are recorded successful moves, not elapsed time. Minimum-move figures are breadth-first searches over cell moves (turn cost ignored): reference points, not proof that a route is optimal.

05

Map evolution

one card per dump

Map snapshots appear here after analysis.

Each dump is the robot's map at the moment it was printed, with the route driven up to that line. Wall knowledge between dumps is not in the log, so it is not shown.
